IronPulse Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 (last updated 2026-10-23)

IronPulse is an offline gym workout log for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. It applies to the IronPulse app published on Google Play by the developer named below ("we", "us").

  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • Your workouts stay on your phone. Everything you log is stored only in the app on your device. We have no servers and never receive your workouts, body weight or settings.
  • Ads are provided by Google. The free version shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which collects some device data (described below). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings.
  • Backups are yours. Android may back up the app's data to your own Google account, and you can save backup files wherever you choose.

Information stored on your device

IronPulse saves what you enter so it can show your history, records and progress:

  • workouts, exercises, sets (weight, reps, time, distance, effort), notes and routines;
  • custom exercises you create;
  • body weight and, if you enter it, body fat percentage;
  • settings such as units, weekly goal, rest times, reminders and theme.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else by the app, and we cannot see it. The app works fully offline.

Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The free version of IronPulse shows banner ads on some screens, an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a workout, and optional videos you can choose to watch to unlock Pro features for 24 hours. These ads are served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Your workout data is never shared with Google or any advertiser.

Your consent and choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, IronPulse uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent (or offer an opt-out) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google › Ads).
  • IronPulse asks Google for ads rated no higher than "parental guidance" (PG).
  • IronPulse Pro, when available, removes all ads.

Your consent choice is kept only on your phone and is not restored to a new phone from a backup; you will be asked again there.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy IronPulse Pro, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment details under the Google Payments Privacy Notice. The app receives only a confirmation from Google Play, on your phone, that your Google account owns Pro. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

The app may also ask Google Play to show its standard rating prompt. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play.

Backups and exports

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, Android copies the IronPulse database and settings to your Google account backup, and to a new phone when you set one up. This backup is made by Android, is stored in your Google account (end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock on Android 9 and newer), and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings › Google › Backup.
  • Backup files and CSV export. When you choose Back up or Export CSV, the app creates a file and lets you save it where you choose or share it with an app you choose (for example Google Drive or email). It goes only where you send it.
  • Imports. When you restore a backup or import a Strong CSV file, the app reads the file you pick, on your phone.
  • Sharing a workout. When you share a workout, the app makes the share card image on your phone (with your photo, if you add one) and opens Android's share sheet, or Instagram when you choose Instagram Story or Add stats to my video; it goes only to the app you choose. With Instagram, the app also passes IronPulse's public Meta app ID, which Instagram requires to show which app the post came from; it identifies the app, not you.

IronPulse does not ask for permission to use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. Files are only opened or saved through Android's own file picker, when you choose to. The one exception is Save sticker, which puts the stats sticker in your gallery (see below); Android 10 and newer allow that without a permission.

Photos you add to a share card come from Android's own photo picker or your camera app, and videos you add your stats to come from the photo picker; either gives IronPulse only the photo or video you choose or take. They are edited on your phone and go only where you share them. IronPulse never uploads them and does not keep them with your workouts: it deletes the copy the picker or camera hands it as soon as the photo is on the card. The finished card image you share stays in the app's private cache, which is not backed up, so the app you shared it to can read it. It is replaced the next time you share, and Android clears it over time (Settings › Apps › IronPulse › Storage › Clear cache removes it at once).

When you share to Instagram, IronPulse hands it the card, or your video with the stats sticker, and Instagram reads them after it opens. So they stay in the same private cache for at least an hour, and IronPulse deletes them the next time you open the share screen after that. Save sticker puts the sticker image, which shows only your workout stats, in your phone's gallery (Pictures › IronPulse), where you can delete it like any picture; on Android 9 and older it opens the share sheet instead, so IronPulse never needs a storage permission.

Health and fitness information

Workouts, body weight and body fat percentage are health and fitness information. They stay on your device as described above, are never used for advertising and are never sold or shared. IronPulse does not connect to Health Connect or any other health service.

IronPulse is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and its estimates (such as an estimated one-rep max) are for training guidance only. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme.

Children

IronPulse is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices, retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual workouts and entries in the app, or everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Android backups in your Google account can be deleted in Google Drive (Backups) or your phone's backup settings.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For data collected by Google for ads, use the choices above or Google's tools at My Ad Center.
  • We do not sell personal information.
