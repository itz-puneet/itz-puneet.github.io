Your data and the publishing tool

Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy describes The Ancient Vault Publisher, an owner-operated tool run by Puneet Deshwani for The Ancient Vault, and this public information website.

Google and YouTube information accessed

When the channel owner authorizes the tool, it requests the Google permissions youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. The tool uses them to access:

  • The authorized channel’s ID, name and uploads-playlist information.
  • Video identifiers, titles, descriptions and upload information needed to find or verify its uploads.
  • Video processing status, visibility, scheduled publication time and audience settings.
  • OAuth authorization credentials, including a refresh token, needed to maintain the owner’s authorized connection.

The owner supplies the video files, scripts, titles, descriptions and other publishing settings. The tool also keeps records of topics, publication slots, upload attempts and completed video IDs.

How the information is used

Google API information is used to verify the intended channel, upload approved videos, schedule publication, check results, renew authorized access and reconcile interrupted uploads without creating duplicates. Original scripts, narration and visuals are prepared as part of the owner’s publishing workflow.

The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Google API data is not used by the uploader to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and service providers

OAuth credentials and operational records are stored on the owner’s managed cloud workspace with restricted filesystem access. The public website and its public source repository do not contain OAuth credentials or the private publishing ledger.

The workflow runs using OpenAI Codex and its managed cloud environment. Relevant channel and upload-status information may be processed there to operate the publishing workflow. Video files and publishing metadata are sent to Google/YouTube as required to perform the owner’s authorized uploads.

For spoken narration, original scripts intended for publication and voice-delivery directions are sent to Google’s Gemini speech-generation API through Google AI Studio. YouTube OAuth credentials, private channel API responses and other private account data are not included in these speech requests. The workflow requests stateless processing; Google’s applicable service terms still govern data handling. On the free tier, Google may use submitted narration and generated output to improve its products. See the Gemini API terms.

This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and related technical information for security and service operation under the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages add no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or sign-in forms.

Retention and deletion

Publishing records are retained while needed to operate the owner’s channel, verify results and prevent duplicate uploads. Credentials are kept while the connection is in use. The owner can stop the workflow and remove its stored credentials and records.

To request deletion of information stored by the tool, email puneetytshorts1@gmail.com. Identify the channel and the records concerned; do not send a password or OAuth token. The operator will verify the request and remove the requested stored information, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Access can also be revoked in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized API access; it does not by itself delete videos already uploaded to YouTube or erase the tool’s saved upload records. Uploaded videos can be managed or deleted in YouTube Studio. Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google API Limited Use

The Ancient Vault Publisher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, support or deletion requests, contact Puneet Deshwani at puneetytshorts1@gmail.com.
